Chuck Morrison has kept a count of meetings with "Alignment" in the title. The count is 412.

He has tracked how many of those meetings produced a decision in the meeting itself. The count is 31.

The word comes from mechanics, where alignment is measurable. Wheels are aligned or they are not, and a machine will tell you which. Corporate alignment has no machine. It is reported by the people in the room, usually the ones who most want the meeting to end.

The most aligned team Chuck ever worked on never used the word. They had one owner, one spreadsheet, and a Friday deadline. The team was dissolved in a reorg. The announcement said the change would drive greater alignment.
