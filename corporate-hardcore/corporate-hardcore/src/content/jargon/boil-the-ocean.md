The first time Chuck Morrison heard "boil the ocean," he was asking for a complete inventory of every laptop the company owned. The request was described as boiling the ocean, and he was asked to inventory only the laptops in the main building.

Four years later, the company could not account for 140 laptops during an audit. Most of them were in the other buildings.

Chuck does not consider the phrase wrong. Some oceans should not be boiled. He considers it incomplete. Every time the ocean is declined, someone is quietly accepting the fish that stay in it. That person is almost never in the meeting.

The full inventory was completed the following year. It was described in the announcement as a strategic initiative.
