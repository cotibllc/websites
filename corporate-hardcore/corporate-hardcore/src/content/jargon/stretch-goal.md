Every October, Synergy Corp holds Q4 planning, which is when next year is planned. Chuck Morrison has attended 18 of them.

In each one, someone proposes a number that everyone in the room knows is unreachable. Someone else suggests calling it a stretch goal. There is visible relief. The number goes on the slide.

The slide is then copied into the January kickoff deck by someone who was not in the October meeting. The copy does not include the word "stretch," because there was not room in the text box.

Chuck has watched the same text box turn hope into accountability for 18 years. He no longer objects to stretch goals. He asks for a second text box.
