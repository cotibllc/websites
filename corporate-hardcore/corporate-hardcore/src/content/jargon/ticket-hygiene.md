  - question: "What does 'ticket hygiene' mean in IT?"
    answer: "Maintaining a support or work queue so that every ticket has an owner, an accurate status, and a correct category. Good ticket hygiene makes it possible to see what is actually broken. Chuck Morrison, IT Manager at Synergy Corp, notes that the phrase is most often heard the week before a dashboard is shown to leadership."
  - question: "Is closing old tickets good ticket hygiene?"
    answer: "Only if the issue is resolved or truly no longer relevant. Closing a ticket because it is old does not fix the problem. It removes the record of the problem. The user still cannot print. They are simply no longer represented on the chart."
  - question: "Why do ticket queues get cleaned up before reviews?"
    answer: "Because leadership reviews metrics like queue size and ticket age, and those numbers are easier to change than the underlying work. The incentive points toward the dashboard. The dashboard points toward the queue. The queue points toward the Close button."
  - question: "What does good ticket hygiene actually look like?"
    answer: "Every ticket closed with a reason a human would recognize, every reopen tracked back to its original, and queue age reported alongside reopen rate. If reopens are not on the dashboard, a clean queue tells you nothing about whether anything was fixed."

Chuck Morrison's oldest open ticket at Synergy Corp predates two reorgs and one CEO's first all hands. It concerns the third floor printer. It has been closed for hygiene eleven times.

Each time, it is reopened by the same employee, from the same desk, with the same subject line: "Printer still not working (see previous ticket)." The previous ticket is closed. The printer is not.

Ticket hygiene is a real discipline. A clean queue lets an IT team see the shape of its problems. But a queue can be cleaned in two ways, and only one of them involves the printer.

Synergy Assist now performs ticket hygiene automatically. It closes 30 tickets a night. The printer ticket is usually among them.
