Chuck Morrison has completed required learning on data privacy 18 times. The content has changed twice.

He knows every answer to the final quiz. So does everyone else. The quiz allows unlimited attempts, which means that every employee at Synergy Corp has, on paper, a perfect understanding of data privacy.

One year, the learning platform added a feature that detected when the video was muted. Completion times went up by 40 seconds. Comprehension was not measured, so nobody knows what the 40 seconds bought.

Dana Chen, Gen Z Innovation Intern, has completed the "Welcome to Synergy Corp" new hire module six times, once for every year in the role, because the system still classifies an intern as a new hire. She now helps write the quiz questions.
