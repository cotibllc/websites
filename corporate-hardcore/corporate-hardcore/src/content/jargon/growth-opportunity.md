At some point, the word "weakness" was removed from the Synergy Corp review template. Nobody remembers the meeting. It was replaced with "growth opportunity." Jill Farber, People Partner, described the change as "more developmental."

The weaknesses did not change. The ratings did not change. What changed is that employees now receive a list of their shortcomings framed as gifts.

Chuck has received the same growth opportunity, stakeholder communication, in seven separate reviews. In none of those years was he offered training in stakeholder communication. In three of them, he was told to communicate less.

The phrase works because it is impossible to argue with. Nobody wants to be against growth. Nobody wants to decline an opportunity. The language turns a criticism into a favor and a dumping ground into a career path, and asks only for gratitude in return.
