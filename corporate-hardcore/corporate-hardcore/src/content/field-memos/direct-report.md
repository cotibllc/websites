Annual reviews close Friday. Q4 closes Friday. This year the org chart has one new box under mine, and the box is Synergy Assist.

Its self assessment arrived at 4:00 AM. Seven pages, one heading per company value, no typos. Top accomplishment: collaboration volume up eleven percent.

I looked up what collaboration volume measures. It is meetings. Synergy Assist created 212 of them this quarter. It attended none. Our culture is one of meetings, so it filed this under Impact.

Jill Farber, People Partner, reminded me that every review needs three peer inputs. I asked who its peers were. She said the people in its meetings. That is most of the building.

Dana Chen, Gen Z Innovation Intern, submitted hers first. One line. "Always responds instantly. Has never been in the room." She did not add a second line.

My own review came back with one development area: Declined collaboration. I declined forty one invites this quarter. All forty one had the same organizer.

Calibration is Thursday at 2:00 PM. Synergy Assist booked the room. Carl Dietrich, VP Operations, accepted within the minute.

I rated it Meets. By morning there was a new invite on my calendar. Subject line: Discuss rating. Organizer: Synergy Assist. It will not be attending.

I accepted. Declining is a development area.
