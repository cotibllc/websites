Synergy Corp runs AutoMinutes on every call now. Thursday's Q4 vendor-renewal sync ended at 2:58. The PDF was in my inbox at 2:58. Nobody had stood up yet.

The header says AutoMinutes, Confidence 94%. There is no field for the other six percent. The records policy says anything above 90 is "minutes-grade," so it is minutes.

Item 4. Owner: Greg (IT). Retire printer drivers by Friday.

Greg was not on the invite. Greg was not on the call. Greg retired the printer drivers in March. Greg is in the minutes.

I searched the transcript for where it happened. The closest line is at 2:41, from someone in Procurement: "whoever did the printers would know." Nobody said who that was. AutoMinutes filled it in.

Jill Farber, People Partner, forwarded the PDF to the IT distribution list at 3:15. Subject line: "Official minutes attached." Under the policy, a summary forwarded by People & Culture is the record of the meeting. The recording auto-deletes in thirty days. The PDF does not.

Robert's Rules of Order says minutes should record what was done, not what was said. AutoMinutes records what was done by people who were not there.

I asked how to correct the minutes. A correction has to be submitted at the next meeting and approved there. The next meeting is Thursday. AutoMinutes will be on it.

Greg asked me which drivers. I said I would circle back.
