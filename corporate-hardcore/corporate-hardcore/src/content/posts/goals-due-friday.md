The goal setting window for 2026 opened Monday. It closes Friday. Year end reviews start the week after, and they rate us against these goals.

The window was scheduled for February. The reorg moved it to June. The system migration moved it to November. The announcement called this a fresh start.

I opened the portal and entered my first goal: retire the old file servers. I retired them in August. The form asked for a target date. I entered August 28. The portal said target dates cannot be in the past.

Under the Goal Integrity Policy, goals must be forward looking and may not be created after the work is complete. Jill Farber, People Partner, sent a summary at 10:02 AM. Backdated goals would let employees set targets they already knew they had met. The policy protects the integrity of the rating.

I understood. I deleted August 28 and looked for the latest date the portal would accept. It was December 31.

So I have six weeks.

I built a new list. Things I could start and finish before December 31, measurable, aligned to a company value. Fix the conference room displays. Close the printer tickets older than ninety days. Update the onboarding checklist. My manager approved all three in eleven minutes. His note said Good stretch.

The file servers are not on the list. Neither is the badge system cutover, or the Saturday in July when the storage array failed and I was there until two in the morning. Those happened in a year that, according to the portal, had no goals.

Dana Chen, Gen Z Innovation Intern, has the cubicle across from mine. Six years, employee 00612. Her one goal is Survive Q4. Target date December 31. The portal accepted it on the first try. It meets every requirement.

At the bottom of the review form there is a box labeled Additional Context. I put the file servers there, and the badge cutover, and the Saturday in July. The box has a limit of 500 characters. I used 498. I do not know who reads that box.

Before I closed the portal I added one more goal. Complete 2026 goal setting by Friday. I marked it complete.

It is the first goal I have finished this year.
