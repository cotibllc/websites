The Thanksgiving lunch was cancelled on Tuesday. The email said that in the current environment we are all being asked to steward resources thoughtfully. The lunch was $640. It was a tray of turkey sandwiches and two pies.

On Wednesday, Finance sent the Q4 close reminder. Unspent department budget does not roll over. Funds not committed by December 15 are returned, and next year's budget is based on this year's actual spend.

My team has $4,200 left.

On Thursday, the year end review form opened. It has a new competency this year, between Collaboration and Customer Focus. Resource Stewardship. The definition is two sentences long: Demonstrates responsible use of company resources. Aligns spend to business priorities.

I read the Resource Stewardship Guidelines, section 4, Year End Spend. It says managers should avoid discretionary purchases in the final weeks of the quarter. It also says managers should plan budgets accurately, since underspend signals overbudgeting.

I asked my manager which rule applied. He said both. He said use good judgment.

So I made two lists. List one: spend the $4,200 by December 15. Replace the six monitors that flicker, buy the spare laptop batteries we keep borrowing from Facilities, renew the cable tester. Keep next year's budget. List two: spend nothing. Show restraint. Lose $4,200 next year, which is the monitors, the batteries and the cable tester, again.

Dana Chen, Gen Z Innovation Intern, six years, employee 00612, asked if the lunch money went back into the budget. I checked. It went to a cost center I do not have access to.

On Friday I wrote my self assessment for Resource Stewardship. The box allows 300 characters. I wrote that I had evaluated all options against business priorities. That is true. It used a fifth of the box.

I submitted the purchase order at 4:51 PM. Or I deleted it. The guidelines say managers should not disclose pending spend decisions before the quarter closes, so I will leave it there.

My manager rates the competency in January, after the budget numbers come back. He will know which list I picked. He has not told me which one he would have picked.

The lunch would have been $640 on either list.
