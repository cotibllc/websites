The evidence portal found my entire year. My reviewer can see April.

I had been looking forward to this part of annual reviews. The portal now uses AI agents to collect completed work from our ticket system and match it to our goals. No more searching my sent folder for proof that something happened in February. I connected my account, checked the permissions, and let the import run.

It found the access migration. Four hundred and twelve accounts moved, old access removed, completion report attached. That took three weekends in March. The portal had the right dates and the right files. I checked all four hundred and twelve rows before I clicked Continue.

The next screen asked for manager validation.

Under the Contribution Continuity Standard, evidence must be validated by the manager responsible when the work occurred. I understand that. My current reviewer should not have to take my word for everything. There should be a record.

The manager who approved the migration moved in the reorg. His name still appears on the completion report. It no longer appears in the manager dropdown.

I searched by last name, first name, and employee number. The dropdown returned no results. The help text suggested checking the spelling.

I checked it against his signature on the report.

There was an option for work completed without a manager. Selecting it required me to certify that I had operated without management oversight. I had received eleven requests for status updates during the migration. I left the box unchecked.

Jill Farber, People Partner, sent me the exception instructions. A current manager could validate historical work after receiving confirmation from the historical manager. The confirmation had to be entered through the portal. Email attachments were not accepted as confirmation because the portal could not verify who had sent them.

I forwarded the instructions to my former manager. He replied, “Happy to help. Send the link.”

I sent it.

He sent back a screenshot of a page saying he did not have permission to view my evidence.

I opened an access ticket. The service desk needed approval from the owner of the review cycle. The review cycle owner asked whether granting access would allow a manager outside my reporting line to view my review. I said that was the request.

The ticket is with Privacy now.

My reviewer has already booked our conversation. The portal preview shows the laptop replacement I finished in April, after the reorg. It took two afternoons. Beside the migration, the status is “Completed, pending validation.” That status excludes the item from the review packet.

I asked whether I should bring a printed copy. My reviewer said we should use the packet so we were both working from the same information.

I printed the migration report anyway. The room on the invitation has a table for six. There will be two of us.

I will put it in the middle.
