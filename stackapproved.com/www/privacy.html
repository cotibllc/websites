Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

StackApproved is a brand of COTIB LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company (“COTIB LLC,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what we collect on stackapproved.com, why, and who helps us process it.

Who runs this site

COTIB LLC, 155 Willowbrook Blvd, Ste 110 #2800, Wayne, NJ 07470, United States. Privacy questions go to chuck@stackapproved.com.

What the brief form collects

When you send a brief, the form sends us:

  • your name (required)
  • your work email (required)
  • your company or product (optional)
  • the offer you are interested in (optional)
  • the text of your brief (required)
  • a Cloudflare Turnstile token, which shows the security check passed

The form also has a hidden field that people never see. If it is filled in, we treat the message as spam and drop it.

We do not ask for payment details on this site. Please do not send passwords or confidential information through the form.

How we use it

We use what you send to read your brief, reply to you, send the hook ideas you asked for, and talk about a possible project. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell or rent your information.

Where it goes

Your brief goes to a small server function hosted by Vercel. That function asks Cloudflare to confirm the security check, then emails your brief to our business inbox through Resend, an email delivery service, with your email address set as the reply address. The website does not save briefs in a database. The copy we keep is that email.

Services that handle your data

  • Vercel hosts the site and the form function.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site for security and delivery, and runs the Turnstile check on the form. To do that it processes your IP address and browser signals.
  • Resend delivers the form email to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.

Each of these services may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and request times, under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies, and our own code does not set cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies or use similar signals to protect the site and run the Turnstile check. If we add analytics later, we will update this page first.

How long we keep it

We keep brief emails for as long as we need them to reply, run any project that follows, and keep normal business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing chuck@stackapproved.com. Depending on where you live, local law may give you more rights, and we will honor them as the law requires.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

The site runs over HTTPS, and the form uses the Turnstile check and the hidden spam field to block automated submissions. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive details out of the form.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
