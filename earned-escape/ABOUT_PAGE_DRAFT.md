### 2. A Lifetime of Travel (The Origin)
**Headline:** Exploring the world since I was six.
**Body:**
My parents instilled a love of travel in me before I could fully understand how big the world was. Since I was six years old, I’ve been on the move. I’ve traveled solo, I’ve traveled with my wife, and I’ve planned the incredibly complex, multi-generational trips that happen when your kids start having kids of their own.

I’ve made the mistakes so you don’t have to. I know what an itinerary looks like when it’s too packed, and I know the magic that happens when you leave just enough room to breathe. 

### 3. The Precision Mindset (The Methodology)
**Headline:** 30 years of IT precision, applied to your vacation.
**Body:**
For three decades, I built a career in corporate information technology. In that world, the difference between success and catastrophic failure comes down to one thing: attention to detail. 

I architect travel the exact same way I used to architect complex IT systems. I look for the failure points. I mitigate the risks. I don’t believe in "winging it" when it comes to the logistics, because perfectly executed logistics are what give you the freedom to actually relax. You aren’t just getting a travel agent; you’re getting a systems thinker who treats your investment with the respect it deserves.

### 4. The Bucket List (China Trip 2026)

In January 2026, I checked off one of my biggest personal bucket list items: a 12-day journey through China, from Beijing to Xi'an to Chongqing. Standing on the Great Wall, seeing the Terracotta Warriors—it was a powerful reminder of *why* we travel.
