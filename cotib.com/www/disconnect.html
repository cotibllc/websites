Disconnect QuickBooks

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This page explains how to disconnect COTIB LLC’s private QuickBooks Online app (GrokIntegration) from your Intuit company.

Disconnect in QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as the company admin.
  2. Open Settings (gear) → Apps / connected apps (or Intuit Account → Sign in & security → Apps).
  3. Find GrokIntegration (or the COTIB / Grok Bot QuickBooks connection).
  4. Choose Disconnect / Revoke access.

Disconnect on the Grok Bot side

Ask Grok Bot to clear Production QuickBooks environment credentials / tokens for this connector, or remove QUICKBOOKS_* secrets from the connector environment and restart so no further API calls succeed.

After disconnect

The App can no longer read or write your QuickBooks Online data until you reconnect via the Connect flow. Historical data already in QuickBooks stays in Intuit’s systems.
