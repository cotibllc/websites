Fractional CTO & Advisory Services

Helping scaling companies build, optimize, and secure their technology foundations. Bringing executive technology expertise without the full-time overhead.

Pillar 01

Fractional CTO & Advisory

High-level strategic guidance to align technology investments directly with business growth, ensuring scale, team alignment, and clear roadmap execution.

Engagement Areas

  • Technical Roadmapping: Defining architecture evolutionary plans that map to your 1-3 year business goals.
  • Engineering Leadership Mentorship: Developing internal engineering directors, leads, and architects into strategic leaders.
  • Vendor & Tool Selection Governance: Managing vendor reviews, negotiating enterprise contracts, and eliminating SaaS waste.
  • Board & Due Diligence Advisory: Preparing technical presentations for boards, investors, or representing the company during fundraising audits.
Pillar 02

AI Platform Engineering

Embedding production-grade generative AI into your architectures and internal workflows with strict budget controls and data privacy models.

Engagement Areas

  • Azure OpenAI Implementation: Designing scalable service architectures supporting first-to-market AI integrations.
  • Enterprise AI Service Gateway: Establishing central access keys, cost monitoring, and traffic throttling for LLM APIs (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic).
  • AI Agent Orchestration: Building custom internal workspace agents (e.g., Azure AI Foundry + Teams) to streamline operational alignment and embedding intelligence in daily communications.
  • AI Policy & Governance: Drafting enterprise guidelines for developers using AI code-completion tools securely without leaking IP.
Pillar 03

Platform Engineering & Scale

Overhauling delivery pipelines to increase engineering velocity, reduce operational friction, and establish self-service platforms for developers.

Engagement Areas

  • Developer Platform Engineering: Standardizing CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration, and self-service provisioning to reduce developer setup times.
  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Auditing and refactoring Terraform, Ansible, or Bicep setups to eliminate drift and build reproducible environments.
  • CI/CD Pipeline Optimization: Speeding up deployment checks, unit testing, and image compiles, achieving up to 75% faster shipping times.
  • Global Operations Insourcing: Guiding team setup in alternative time zones (e.g. Costa Rica, Bangalore), implementing follow-the-sun operational structures, and reducing required headcounts by up to 60% with internal talent pipelines.
Pillar 04

FinOps & Cost Control

Establishing systematic cost control across multi-cloud budgets, delivering major savings without degrading performance or reliability metrics.

Engagement Areas

  • Cloud Spend Audits & Baselines: Analyzing invoice granularities in AWS, Azure, or GCP to identify anomalies, orphan resources, and over-provisioned instances.
  • FinOps Governance & Automation: Building alerting policies, auto-shutdown schedules, and tag enforcement tools.
  • Budget Management Optimization: Structuring resource commitment policies (Savings Plans, Reserved Instances) yielding substantial annual savings on multi-million dollar budgets.
