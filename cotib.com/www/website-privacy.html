Website privacy

Effective October 30, 2026

This notice explains what COTIB LLC collects when you visit cotib.com or use its contact form, and who processes it for us.

Two parts of the site have their own notices: the free Do the Next Thing sampler (cotib.com/do-the-next-thing/privacy) and our private QuickBooks integration app (cotib.com/privacy).

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people read. It sets two cookies on cotib.com, _ga and _ga_7C8Q4SNYQQ. They hold a random ID, not your name or email address, and last up to two years unless you clear them.

Google keeps this analytics data for 14 months, then deletes it. Google signals is turned off, so visits are not linked to Google accounts. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, and it is not linked to Google Ads.

Cookie choices

If you visit from the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or we can’t tell where you are, a banner asks for your choice first. Google Analytics does not load, and sets no cookies, until you choose Accept. If you choose Reject, it stays off. Elsewhere, Google Analytics loads when the page opens.

We store your choice in your browser so we don’t ask on every page. You can change it at any time with Cookie choices at the bottom of every page. Choosing Reject also removes the Google Analytics cookies. You can also install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on, or block or clear cookies for cotib.com in your browser settings. The site works the same without them.

Google Analytics does not run on the Do the Next Thing confirm, download and unsubscribe pages.

The contact form

When you send the form on cotib.com/contact, we receive your name, email address and message, plus your organization, phone number and the service you picked if you fill them in. The form sends them to us as an email through Resend, with your address as the reply-to, so we can answer you. You get no email from the form itself.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks the form for bots. It processes data such as your IP address and browser signals to do this. To slow down repeat tries, the form allows about 5 tries a minute from one connection. That count is kept in memory for a minute and isn’t saved. The log lines our form service writes don’t include your name, email address or message.

When you email us directly, we receive your email address and anything you write to us.

Who processes it for us

  • Google Analytics: measures visits, as described above. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Fonts: serves the fonts on our pages, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.
  • Resend: delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare: our network provider, so it handles every request to the site, including your IP address. It also provides Turnstile.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and runs the contact form service.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Contact form messages and emails to us: only as long as we need them to respond. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Resend email logs: 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing customerservice@cotib.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to restrict or object to processing and to ask for your data in a portable format. Our legal basis for Google Analytics is your consent, given or refused in the cookie banner and withdrawn at any time with Cookie choices. We use contact form messages to answer the request you sent us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, you can ask what personal information we collect and ask us to delete it. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

Do Not Track: our pages don’t change behavior based on Do Not Track signals. Use Cookie choices or the opt-out add-on instead.
