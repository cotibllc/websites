Privacy notice: Do the Next Thing

This notice explains what COTIB LLC collects when you use the Do the Next Thing page at cotib.com/do-the-next-thing, sign up for the free sampler, or download it. For our QuickBooks integration app, see cotib.com/privacy.

What we collect and why

When you sign up for the free sampler:

  • Your email address and your consent record: the version of the signup wording you agreed to, when you signed up, when you clicked the confirm link, and a hashed version of your IP address (a one-way code, not the raw IP).
  • We use a two-step signup: we only add you to our list after you click the confirm link in your email.
  • Signups are checked by Cloudflare Turnstile to stop bots. Turnstile processes data such as your IP address and browser signals to do this.
  • We use your email to send the confirm link and the sampler. Your consent also covers up to 3 short emails about the paid Pack. We aren't sending those yet. You can unsubscribe any time.
  • To slow down repeat tries, the signup form allows about 10 tries a minute from one connection. That count is kept in memory for a minute and isn't saved.

When you email us: your email address and anything you write to us.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Resend: sends our emails and stores the free-sampler email list, including your consent record and whether you've unsubscribed.
  • Vercel: hosts this page and runs the signup, confirm, download, and unsubscribe service (serverless functions), and stores the sampler file.
  • Cloudflare: provides Turnstile.

How long we keep it

  • Unconfirmed signups: we don't store them. The confirm link in your email works for 48 hours, and you're only added to our list if you click it.
  • Free sampler download links: 30 days.
  • Sampler list membership and consent record: kept on your contact in Resend for as long as the contact exists.
  • After you unsubscribe: we keep your contact in Resend, marked unsubscribed, so we don't email you again. We don't delete it. If you sign up again later, you're only added back after you click a new confirm link.
  • The log lines our signup service writes hold only one-way codes, never your raw email address or IP address.
  • Resend email logs: 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe: use the link in any of our emails. It takes effect right away.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: email customerservice@cotib.com. If you ask us to delete your sampler contact, we'll delete it from Resend. After that we no longer have a record that you unsubscribed, but you're still only added back if you sign up and click a new confirm link.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your data. Email us and we'll help.
  • Do Not Track: our pages don't change behavior based on Do Not Track signals. We don't track you across other sites.
