Who it's for

Who it's for

  • You already know your next step, on a project, a business, or a piece of work you care about.
  • You stall anyway: the inbox, tidying, one more tutorial, one more round of research.
  • You use an AI chat app, or you're willing to paste a prompt into one.
  • You'll start a 10-minute timer and stop when it ends.

Who it isn't for

  • You don't know your next step yet. These prompts assume you do.
  • You want to understand why you stall. None of the prompts go there.
  • You're looking for a framework, a productivity system, or a course.
  • You want someone to coach you live. This is a prompt pack and a kit, not a person.
