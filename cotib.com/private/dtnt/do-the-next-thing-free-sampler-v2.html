The prompts

1.1 The first ugly version
START: you haven't touched it yet.
I need to start [the thing] and I haven't begun. Give me ONE move I can finish in under 10 minutes that leaves a rough first piece on the page or screen. No research, no setup, no tools to install. Tell me exactly what to type or make first, and what "done" looks like. One sentence of instruction, one sentence of done. Nothing else.
3.1 No catch-up
RESTART AFTER A SKIP: you missed a day, a week, or longer.
I skipped [the thing] for [how long]. Don't give me a catch-up plan. Give me one move under 10 minutes that puts me back in it today, as if the skip never happened. Tell me what to do and what proof I'll have when I'm done.
4.3 Close the tabs
DECIDE: you're stuck between options and researching instead.
I've been researching [the decision] instead of making it. Tell me to close the research, then write my choice in one sentence, then take one 10-minute action that makes the choice real (send it, book it, write it, start it). Tell me exactly what that action is.
If the answer comes back too big, reply

"Too big. Smaller. One move, under 10 minutes, no research."
